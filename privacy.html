隐私政策 · Privacy Policy

生效日期 Effective date：2026-10-31 · 最近更新 Last updated：2026-10-31

1. 简要说明

「汉字大作战」（HanziBattle，Bundle ID：com.hanzibattle.app，以下称「本 App」）是一款面向 6–8 岁儿童的汉字游戏，在 App Store 的「儿童」（Kids）分类上架。本 App 由 Xu Haiming（以下称「我们」或「开发者」）开发。

我们不收集任何个人信息，也不收集任何其他数据。本 App 没有账号、没有广告、没有统计分析、没有第三方 SDK，也没有我们自己的服务器。游戏进度只保存在你的设备上，我们无法看到。

2. 我们不收集的内容

  • 不要求注册或登录，不收集姓名、邮箱、电话、地址、照片、通讯录、位置、设备标识符或广告标识符。
  • 不含任何广告，也不做任何追踪（不使用 App Tracking Transparency，不与任何第三方共享数据）。
  • 不含任何统计、崩溃上报或其他第三方 SDK。
  • 不提供聊天、评论、上传等用户之间互动或用户生成内容的功能。
  • 不使用麦克风、摄像头、相机胶卷或定位。

本 App 随附的《隐私清单》（Privacy Manifest）也声明：不追踪、不收集数据类型。

3. 保存在你设备上的内容

以下内容只存放在你的设备上，不会发送给我们或任何第三方：

  • 游戏进度：通关星星、已收集的字、合字图鉴、常错的字等，保存在 App 的本地文件中。
  • 设置与提示状态：每天可玩时长、音效与语音音量、是否已看过教学提示等，保存在系统的 UserDefaults 中。
  • 家长验证状态：答错次数、锁定时间与当前题目，保存在 UserDefaults 中，以免通过重复打开来换题。
  • 购买缓存标记：一个「是否已验证过解锁」的是/否标记，保存在系统钥匙串（Keychain）中，仅用于让 App 在离线时显示已解锁状态。它不包含任何个人信息，启动时会与 Apple 重新核实。

根据你设备的设置，这些本地数据可能包含在你自己的设备备份中（例如 iCloud 备份或电脑备份）。备份由你和 Apple 管理，我们无法访问。

4. 网络连接

本 App 联网只用于以下两件事，且都由 Apple 提供：

  • App Store / StoreKit：查看价格、购买、恢复购买和验证一次性解锁。付款全程由 Apple 处理，我们看不到也不会保存你的付款信息、Apple 账户信息或家庭成员信息。
  • Apple 按需资源（On-Demand Resources）：解锁后，第 7 章起的关卡内容与配音由 Apple 的服务器托管并下载到设备。免费章节不会发起这类下载。

这些连接适用 Apple 的隐私政策。我们可能通过 App Store Connect 收到 Apple 提供的汇总销售与下载报告，这些报告不会识别具体的用户或儿童。

5. 家长验证与外部链接

购买、恢复购买以及所有会离开本 App 的链接（如本隐私政策与使用条款），都需要先通过家长验证：一道成年人能轻松回答的简单数学题。通过后链接才会在系统浏览器中打开。

本网站为静态页面，不含 Cookie、统计或第三方内容。网站托管在 GitHub Pages 上，与任何网站托管方一样，GitHub 可能依其自身的隐私声明处理访问请求的技术信息（例如 IP 地址）；我们无法获得这些信息。

6. 儿童隐私

本 App 为儿童设计，并遵循 App Store「儿童」分类的要求。我们不会有意收集任何人的个人信息，包括 13 岁以下儿童（美国 COPPA）以及欧盟/英国等地适用 GDPR 儿童同意年龄以下的儿童。由于本 App 不收集个人信息，也就没有需要经过父母同意才能处理的数据。

  • 没有广告，也没有基于行为的广告或画像。
  • 没有账号、聊天或社交功能，孩子无法与他人联系。
  • 购买与外部链接由家长验证保护；每天可玩时长可由家长在设置页调整。

如果你认为孩子通过本 App 向我们提供了个人信息，请联系我们，我们会尽快协助处理。

7. 数据保留与删除

我们不持有你的任何数据，因此没有需要我们保留或删除的内容。本地数据的保留与删除由你控制：

  • 清除进度：在 App 的「设置」中选择「清除游戏进度」，会清除星星、已收集的字和合字图鉴。
  • 删除 App：从设备上删除本 App，会移除其本地进度、设置以及已下载的内容。
  • 钥匙串标记：iOS 可能在 App 被删除后仍保留钥匙串中的条目。该标记只是一个是/否值，不含个人信息，并会在重新安装后与 Apple 核实。

8. 你的权利

根据你所在地区的法律（如 GDPR、CCPA/CPRA），你可能享有访问、更正、删除个人信息等权利。由于我们不收集或持有个人信息，我们没有可提供或删除的内容；我们也不出售或「共享」个人信息。如有疑问，欢迎随时联系我们。

9. 安全

因为我们不收集或传输数据，没有可被我们的服务泄露的用户数据。付款安全由 Apple 负责；本地数据受设备自身的系统安全保护。

10. 政策变更

我们可能不时更新本政策，例如在 App 功能或相关法律发生变化时。更新后的版本会发布在本页面，并更新页首的「最近更新」日期。如果变更会实质性地影响儿童的隐私，我们会在 App 更新说明中提示。继续使用本 App 即表示接受更新后的政策。

English

1. Summary

HanziBattle (汉字大作战, bundle ID com.hanzibattle.app, the "App") is a Chinese-character game for children aged 6–8, listed in the App Store Kids Category. It is developed by Xu Haiming ("we", "us", the "Developer").

We do not collect any personal information, or any other data. The App has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no third-party SDKs and no server of our own. Game progress is stored only on your device, and we cannot see it.

2. What we do not collect

  • No sign-up or log-in. We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, addresses, photos, contacts, location, device identifiers or advertising identifiers.
  • No advertising and no tracking (the App does not use App Tracking Transparency and shares no data with third parties).
  • No analytics, crash-reporting or other third-party SDKs.
  • No chat, comments, uploads or any other user-to-user interaction or user-generated content.
  • No use of the microphone, camera, photo library or location services.

The Privacy Manifest bundled with the App likewise declares: no tracking, no collected data types.

3. What is stored on your device

The following stays on your device and is never sent to us or to any third party:

  • Game progress: stars earned, characters collected, the character-merge collection, frequently missed characters and similar, stored in a local file inside the App.
  • Settings and hint state: daily play limit, sound and voice volume, whether tutorial hints have been seen and similar, stored in the system's UserDefaults.
  • Parental-gate state: the number of wrong answers, the lock-out time and the current question, stored in UserDefaults so that reopening the gate cannot be used to get a new question.
  • Purchase-cache flag: a single yes/no value, "unlock was verified", stored in the system Keychain. It only lets the App show the unlocked state while offline. It contains no personal information and is re-checked with Apple at launch.

Depending on your device settings, this local data may be included in your own device backups (for example iCloud or computer backups). Backups are managed by you and Apple; we cannot access them.

4. Network use

The App uses the network only for two things, both provided by Apple:

  • App Store / StoreKit: showing the price, purchasing, restoring purchases and verifying the one-time unlock. Payment is handled entirely by Apple. We never see or store your payment details, Apple Account details or Family Sharing information.
  • Apple On-Demand Resources: after unlocking, the level content and voice audio for chapter 7 onward are hosted by Apple and downloaded to your device. The free chapters never trigger these downloads.

These connections are subject to Apple's Privacy Policy. We may receive aggregated sales and download reports from Apple through App Store Connect; these do not identify individual users or children.

5. Parental gate and external links

Purchases, restoring purchases and every link that leaves the App (such as this Privacy Policy and the Terms of Use) are protected by a parental gate: a simple math question that an adult can easily answer. Only after passing it does a link open in the system browser.

This website is static: it uses no cookies, analytics or third-party content. It is hosted on GitHub Pages; like any web host, GitHub may process technical request data (such as IP addresses) under its own privacy statement, and we do not receive that information.

6. Children's privacy

The App is designed for children and follows the requirements of the App Store Kids Category. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13 (US COPPA) and children under the applicable age of digital consent under the GDPR (EU/UK). Because the App collects no personal information, there is no data that would require parental consent to process.

  • No advertising, and no behavioral advertising or profiling.
  • No accounts, chat or social features; a child cannot contact anyone through the App.
  • Purchases and external links are protected by the parental gate, and parents can adjust the daily play limit in Settings.

If you believe a child has provided personal information to us through the App, please contact us and we will help promptly.

7. Data retention and deletion

We hold none of your data, so there is nothing for us to retain or delete. You control local data yourself:

  • Clear progress: in the App's Settings choose "Clear game progress" (清除游戏进度) to remove stars, collected characters and the merge collection.
  • Delete the app: deleting the App from your device removes its local progress, settings and downloaded content.
  • Keychain flag: iOS may keep Keychain items after an app is deleted. This flag is only a yes/no value, contains no personal information, and is re-checked with Apple after reinstalling.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or CCPA/CPRA), you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information. Because we neither collect nor hold personal information, there is nothing for us to provide or delete, and we do not sell or "share" personal information. If you have questions, please contact us at any time.

9. Security

Because we do not collect or transmit data, there is no user data that our services could leak. Payment security is Apple's responsibility; local data is protected by your device's own system security.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time, for example when the App's features or applicable laws change. The updated version will be published on this page and the "Last updated" date at the top will change. If a change materially affects children's privacy, we will say so in the App's release notes. Continued use of the App means you accept the updated policy.
