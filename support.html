常见问题

怎样解锁第 7 章及之后的关卡？

第 1–6 章永久免费。点击被锁住的第 7 章（或之后章节）的关卡，会出现解锁页面；点击「解锁全部关卡」按钮，通过家长验证后，按 App Store 的提示完成一次性购买即可。价格由 App Store 在购买前显示。购买一次，不是订阅，不会自动续费。

怎样恢复购买？
  1. 确认设备登录的是当初购买时使用的 Apple 账户（或同一家庭共享组）。
  2. 在解锁页面或「设置」页点击「恢复购买」（已解锁时设置页不显示此项）。
  3. 通过家长验证后，App 会向 App Store 核实并重新解锁，并重新下载付费章节。

如果仍无法恢复，请先检查网络和 Apple 账户，再通过下方邮箱联系我们。

可以离线玩吗？

可以。第 1–6 章随 App 一起安装，无需联网。解锁后，付费章节需要先联网下载一次；下载完成的章节可以离线玩。联网只用于 App Store 的购买/验证和 Apple 的内容下载。

下载需要多少流量和空间？

解锁后需要下载的付费章节内容总计约 400 MB（由 Apple 托管，分为 6 个内容包），建议使用 Wi-Fi，并确保设备有足够的可用空间。下载失败时可重试；如果设备空间不足，系统可能清理未使用的内容，之后联网时 App 会重新下载。

「询问购买」（Ask to Buy）是什么？家长没有批准怎么办？

如果孩子的 Apple 账户开启了「询问购买」，购买请求会发送给家庭组织者，需要家长批准。批准后 App 会自动解锁。如果没有收到批准通知或请求被拒绝，可以在解锁页面选择重新请求。

家长验证是什么？

购买、恢复购买以及所有打开外部链接（如隐私政策和使用条款）的操作，都需要先通过家长验证，它是一道成年人能轻松回答的简单数学题。答错多次会暂时锁定一小段时间。

怎样申请退款？

所有付款与退款均由 Apple 处理，我们无法直接退款。请访问 reportaproblem.apple.com，登录后选择该购买项目并提交申请。如果购买被退款或撤销，付费章节会重新锁定。

家庭共享怎么用？

这个解锁项目已开启家庭共享。家庭组里的成员在各自设备上，可使用同一个 Apple 家庭组，通过「恢复购买」获得解锁。具体规则以 Apple 为准。

你们会收集孩子的数据吗？怎样清除游戏进度？

不会。本 App 没有账号、广告、统计或第三方 SDK，进度只保存在设备上。详见隐私政策。要清除进度，可在 App 的「设置」中选择「清除游戏进度」，或直接删除 App（会清除本地进度、设置和已下载内容）。

怎样限制孩子每天玩的时间？

在 App 的「设置」中可以选择每天可玩的时长（10、15、20、30 分钟或不限）。用完后会出现一个温和的结束页，当天不能再玩。

我发现了内容错误（字义、例句、拼音、配音）。

本 App 的部分内容由 AI 辅助生成，并正在由母语人士审核，所以我们非常感谢你的反馈。请把字和关卡位置，以及你认为正确的内容，发送到下方邮箱。

支持哪些设备？

支持 iPhone 和 iPad，系统需 iOS 17 或更高版本。

Frequently asked questions

How do I unlock chapter 7 and beyond?

Chapters 1–6 are free forever. Tap a level in the locked chapter 7 (or later) to open the unlock screen, tap the unlock button ("解锁全部关卡", unlock all levels), pass the parental gate, and complete the one-time purchase in the App Store sheet. The App Store shows the price before you buy. It is a single purchase, not a subscription, and never renews.

How do I restore my purchase?
  1. Make sure the device is signed in with the Apple Account used for the purchase (or is in the same Family Sharing group).
  2. Tap "Restore Purchases" on the unlock screen or in Settings (Settings hides this row once unlocked).
  3. After the parental gate, the App checks with the App Store, unlocks again and re-downloads the paid chapters.

If it still does not work, check your connection and Apple Account first, then email us using the address below.

Can I play offline?

Yes. Chapters 1–6 are installed with the App and need no connection. After unlocking, the paid chapters must be downloaded once while online; downloaded chapters can then be played offline. The network is used only for App Store purchases and verification and for Apple's content downloads.

How much data and storage do the downloads need?

After unlocking, the paid chapters' content is about 400 MB in total (hosted by Apple, in 6 content packs). Wi-Fi is recommended, and the device needs enough free space. If a download fails you can retry. If the device runs low on storage, the system may remove unused content, and the App will download it again when you are online.

What is Ask to Buy, and what if a parent has not approved it?

If a child's Apple Account uses Ask to Buy, the purchase request goes to the family organizer for approval. After approval the App unlocks automatically. If you never receive an approval, or it was declined, you can ask again from the unlock screen.

What is the parental gate?

Purchases, restoring purchases and every action that opens an external link (such as the Privacy Policy and Terms of Use) first require passing the parental gate: a simple math question an adult can easily answer. After several wrong answers it locks for a short while.

How do I get a refund?

All payments and refunds are handled by Apple; we cannot refund directly. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in, select the purchase and submit your request. If a purchase is refunded or revoked, the paid chapters lock again.

How does Family Sharing work?

The unlock purchase has Family Sharing enabled. Members of the same Apple Family Sharing group can get the unlock on their own devices with "Restore Purchases". Apple's rules govern the details.

Do you collect my child's data? How do I clear game progress?

No. The App has no accounts, ads, analytics or third-party SDKs, and progress stays on the device. See the Privacy Policy. To clear progress, choose "Clear game progress" (清除游戏进度) in the App's Settings, or delete the App (which removes local progress, settings and downloaded content).

How can I limit how long my child plays each day?

In the App's Settings you can choose a daily play time (10, 15, 20 or 30 minutes, or no limit). When the time is used up, a gentle end screen appears and play stops for the day.

I found a content mistake (meaning, sentence, pinyin or voice).

Parts of the App's content are AI-assisted and are under review by native speakers, so we are grateful for your feedback. Please email the character, the level and what you believe is correct to the address below.

Which devices are supported?

iPhone and iPad running iOS 17 or later.
